Anti-Spam Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

SnapJoy Games ("we", "us", "our") operates the website snapjoygames.com. We have a zero-tolerance policy toward spam. This policy explains our commitment not to advertise or promote this website using unsolicited messages.

1. Our commitment

We do not, and will never, advertise or promote snapjoygames.com, our games, or our services by means of unsolicited messages of any kind. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • unsolicited bulk or commercial email (spam);
  • unsolicited SMS, instant messages, or messages in messengers;
  • unsolicited direct messages or comments on social networks, forums, blogs, or chats;
  • unsolicited calls, faxes, or any other unrequested communications.

2. How we communicate

We only send messages to people who have contacted us first or who have explicitly agreed to receive communications from us. We do not buy, rent, sell, or harvest email addresses or other contact lists, and we do not use automated tools to collect contact information.

Any promotional message we send clearly identifies SnapJoy Games as the sender and gives the recipient an easy way to opt out. Opt-out requests are honored promptly.

3. Third parties and partners

We do not authorize any partner, affiliate, contractor, or other third party to promote snapjoygames.com using unsolicited messages. Any such activity is a violation of our policy and is done without our consent. Partners found to be sending spam on our behalf will have their cooperation with us terminated.

4. Reporting spam

If you have received an unsolicited message that advertises snapjoygames.com or claims to be from SnapJoy Games, please report it to official@snapjoygames.com. Include the message with its full headers if possible. We investigate every report and take appropriate action.

In short: SnapJoy Games never uses spam to advertise this website. If you see spam promoting us, it is not from us — please let us know.
